About Jordyn

Jordyn Brownstein, MA, BA — Registered Psychotherapist (Qualifying), RP(Q)

Practicing under the supervision of Catherine Paul, RP (Ontario Registration #006637). CCPA Member #11261702.

Therapy offers a supportive place to gain insight, clarify what matters, and create meaningful change. My approach is guided by empathy and compassion — I aim to offer a warm, non-judgemental space where trust and authenticity can flourish, whatever you're working through.

I work with individuals of all ages, from preteens through elders, and I'm proud to be LGBTQ2S+ allied, racial justice allied, and welcoming to single parents.

What I Can Help With

ADHD Anxiety Depression Stress Self-Esteem Relationships Identity Issues Life Transitions Codependency Family Conflict Peer Relationships Women's Issues Spirituality

Approaches I Draw From

Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT), Acceptance & Commitment Therapy (ACT), Emotionally-Focused Therapy, Attachment-Based Therapy, Person-Centered Therapy, Family Systems, Internal Family Systems (IFS), Jungian, Adlerian, Existential, Positive Psychology — delivered through a culturally sensitive, strength-based lens.

Format

Online sessions only, serving clients across Ontario.

Cost

$150 per session. Most major Canadian insurance plans accepted. Direct billing available for York University and TMU students.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer in-person sessions?

No — sessions are offered online only, so you can join from wherever is most comfortable for you in Ontario.

Is my information confidential?

Yes. What you share in session is confidential, with the standard exceptions required by law (such as risk of harm to yourself or others).
